# Social icon sources

Downloaded October 18, 2026 from the platforms' own brand packs.
Icons retain original proportions and colors; PNGs are scaled to 112 px for
28 CSS px display (4×), avoiding the multi-megabyte original Instagram file.

- Instagram: `01 Static Glyph/01 Gradient Glyph/Instagram_Glyph_Gradient.png`
  from the logo pack linked by https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/instagram/instagram-brand/ .
- TikTok: `TikTok_Icon_Black_Square.png` from the developer logo pack linked by
  https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/getting-started-design-guidelines .
  Pack: https://sf16-va.tiktokcdn.com/obj/eden-va2/uvzhqeh7nuhd/tt4d/logo-pack.zip .

Trademarks remain their respective owners'. The icons are image-sharing cues
within one Share workout button, not separate posting actions or endorsements.
